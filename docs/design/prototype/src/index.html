Tapcue · project

From screen map to handoff

Tapcue turns designer artwork into a tablet interface for exhibition stands. The pages below follow the work in order: screen map → wireframes → design system → screens → handoff.

Process · documentation, low fidelity

  • 1 · Screen mapEvery screen of the three apps with its route, contents and states, and how the apps connect.
  • 2 · WireframesGreyscale wireframes of every screen at real size, with numbered layout notes.

Design · final

  • Design systemTokens, contrast check and every component with its variants and states.
  • Pages editorThe main editor screen at 1440 × 900: hotspots, uploads, safe area, command log.
  • Targets, Stations, SettingsThe other editor tabs, with empty, error, dialog and demo-mode states.
  • PlayerWhat visitors see on the iPad: artwork, press effects, fade, reconnecting.
  • Demo receiverThe page a reviewer opens next to the player to watch commands arrive.

Handoff

  • Review and handoffOne-off audit, state coverage, contrast and focus, 1280 × 800 layout, app icon and logo, tokens and component inventory.
